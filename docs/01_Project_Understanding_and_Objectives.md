# Lab Resource Utilization Platform

## 1. Project Understanding

### Introduction

Laboratories in universities, research institutions, and industries contain expensive equipment that is often shared among multiple departments. Most institutions still rely on manual registers, spreadsheets, or paper-based systems to manage laboratory resources. This leads to equipment conflicts, inefficient scheduling, underutilization, maintenance delays, and poor resource visibility.

The Lab Resource Utilization Platform is a centralized web application that enables institutions to efficiently manage laboratory equipment, schedule bookings, monitor equipment utilization, track maintenance activities, and generate analytical reports. The platform provides secure role-based access for researchers, lab technicians, lab managers, institution administrators, and system administrators.

The application is developed using React.js for the frontend, Spring Boot for the backend, PostgreSQL as the primary database, and JWT authentication for secure access.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

The primary objective of the Lab Resource Utilization Platform is to optimize the utilization of laboratory resources through a centralized digital platform.

The project aims to:

- Maintain a centralized inventory of laboratory equipment.
- Provide real-time equipment availability.
- Enable researchers to reserve laboratory equipment online.
- Reduce scheduling conflicts between users.
- Improve utilization of expensive laboratory resources.
- Maintain preventive maintenance and calibration records.
- Generate utilization analytics for management.
- Support secure role-based authentication and authorization.
- Improve operational efficiency through automation.
- Facilitate collaboration and resource sharing among departments and institutions.
